About Milly Jay Hotel & Event Center

A welcoming place to stay and celebrate in Punikrom, Sefwi Wiawso, Western North Region, Ghana.

Our Hotel

Comfort, hospitality and a place for your special occasions

Milly Jay Hotel & Event Center is a hotel and event venue in Punikrom, Sefwi Wiawso. The hotel offers 30 air-conditioned rooms, each with a private balcony, a swimming pool, a restaurant and coffee shop, and a bar and lounge — a comfortable base whether you are passing through the Western North Region or staying for a celebration.

Beside the rooms, the hotel runs an event center for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, conferences and other gatherings, bringing guests together for the occasions that matter.

Our hospitality philosophy

We believe a good stay is a simple one: a clean, comfortable room, food and drink on site, and staff who are there when you need them. Our front desk is open 24 hours, and our team is happy to help with anything from an early check-in to planning your event.

What makes Milly Jay special

Every room comes with its own private balcony and air conditioning. Beyond the rooms, guests can swim in the pool, eat at the restaurant and coffee shop, relax at the bar and lounge, or play snooker. And when there is something to celebrate, the hotel’s event center is ready to host it.

What to expect as our guest

A warm welcome at reception, a comfortable room with a balcony, breakfast in the morning, and a pool and restaurant to enjoy during your stay. If you are here for an event, our team will work with you on the details — capacity, packages and catering are confirmed with the hotel for each occasion.

Good to know: the hotel is a cash-only, smoke-free property with no elevators, and pets are not allowed.
