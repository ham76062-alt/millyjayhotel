Welcome to Milly Jay Hotel

A comfortable stay and a place to celebrate in Sefwi Wiawso

Milly Jay Hotel & Event Center sits in Punikrom, Sefwi Wiawso, in the Western North Region — a welcoming stop for travellers, families and anyone marking a special occasion. The hotel has 30 air-conditioned rooms, each with a private balcony, plus a swimming pool, a restaurant and coffee shop, and a bar and lounge.

Alongside the rooms, the hotel runs an event center for weddings, birthdays, corporate events and other gatherings — a venue made for the moments worth celebrating.

  • 30 air-conditioned rooms, all with private balconies
  • Swimming pool, restaurant and coffee shop, bar and lounge
  • Event center for celebrations and gatherings
  • Free Wi-Fi, free self parking and a 24-hour front desk
