Privacy Policy

How Milly Jay Hotel & Event Center handles the information you share with us.

Information you give us

When you send a booking enquiry, an event enquiry or a message through this website, you share your name, phone number and any details you write in the form. That information is sent to the hotel through WhatsApp so the hotel can answer your enquiry and confirm availability.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, confirm a reservation or event, and contact you about your stay or event. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else for marketing.

What we do not collect

This website does not create accounts, does not take payments, and does not store your details in a database. Your enquiry goes directly to the hotel through WhatsApp.

Contact

If you have any questions about your information, call the hotel on 053 153 0999.
